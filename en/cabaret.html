Cabaret

Class, feathers & sparkle

Welcome to the cabaret! Cabaret is a living show that blends dance, theatre and humour — upbeat choreographies and dazzling costumes. Glamorous and elegant, it's the perfect class to build your ease and boost your confidence. You're going to be stunning!

The music ranges from Parisian cabaret classics to today's latest hits. We work in socks or barefoot… and in heels on stage, on show day.
