Your questions, answered

Do I need experience?

No. All levels are welcome. The first class is always free, with no commitment. If you already dance, you can jump straight into our most demanding show choreographies.

What is the commitment?

Adults: per term (no commitment) or per year (better value). Children: yearly enrolment (mid-September to mid-July, excluding school holidays).

How much does it cost?

Adults — 1 class: €250/term or €660/year; 2 classes: €350/term or €900/year. Baby Dance (children): €500/year. First class free. Companies, schools and nurseries on request.

Where are the classes held?

At the Fit & Fresh studio, 15 Avenue de la Faïencerie, L-1510 Luxembourg (Limpertsberg).

What are the times?

All classes are on Wednesday: Baby Dance (ages 3-6) at 5:30 PM, Cabaret at 6:30 PM, Broadway Jazz at 7:30 PM.
