Broadway Jazz

Acting & elegance

Step into the captivating world of Broadway Jazz: we dance to the great musical-theatre hits — Hairspray, Chicago, A Chorus Line, Newsies… — learning lively choreographies that capture all the energy of those musicals.

Whether you're a seasoned dancer or taking your very first steps, the class is designed to be both challenging and fun: a space to refine your technique and stage presence, and to express yourself freely through movement.
