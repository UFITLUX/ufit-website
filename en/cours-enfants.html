Kids' classes

Dancing is all about having fun

The Baby Dance class introduces children aged 3 to 6 to dance in a playful way: we develop their artistic sense through the body, space, time, music and interaction with others. Everything happens through play — danced movements, free expression, imitation, then little choreographies.

The choreographies are renewed regularly, all the way to preparing the big end-of-year show in June.
