One passion, one team, one stage

Born from a deep conviction

U'Fit was born in Luxembourg from a simple observation: there was no open, caring and ambitious artistic space where dance and cabaret would be accessible to everyone. What began as a studio became a genuine community of enthusiasts — shows every year, and many couples coached for their wedding dance.

"We didn't create U'Fit to have a studio. We created it to share a passion." — Meghan, founder

The people who get you dancing

Meghan

Founder & artistic director

A dancer and choreographer, Meghan has been teaching dance in Luxembourg for nearly 10 years. Trained in many styles, she teaches the Cabaret, Broadway Jazz and Baby Dance classes, stages the season's shows and coaches couples for their wedding dance. Still active on stage, she performs every season in shows and musicals. Always smiling, she guides you with her good spirits.

Bikey Seranilla

Professional dancer & choreographer

Trained in classical ballet, contemporary, modern, jazz and Latin dances (samba, cha-cha, rumba, jive), Bikey spent five years in a company before becoming a teacher there, then moving to Europe. A multi-award-winning choreographer, he is the 2021 Asian Music Games champion in the Movement category. His specialty: Theatrical Jazz — he grew up with Chicago, Cats and A Chorus Line.
